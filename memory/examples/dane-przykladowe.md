# Sample data: Pizzeria Amalfi 2025

Sources: `reference/sample-data/` (CSV, DOCX description, PNG chart). Description of the company as a client: `examples/firmy/pizzeria-amalfi.md`.

The portable synthetic transport-company alternative is `backend/sample_data/northstar-logistics-bank-statement-2025.csv`; its profile and seed behavior are documented in `examples/firmy/northstar-logistics.md`.

The organizers say the data is **optional** (we can use our own) and was generated by AI, so it is not always fully realistic.

## CSV file

- `pizzeria-amalfi-bank-statement-2025-v6.csv`: a statement in a format typical of German online banking (similar to MT940).
- Separator `;`, all fields in quotes, decimal comma (`-3200,00`), date in `DD.MM.YY` format.
- 1125 transactions from 01.01.2025–31.12.2025, sorted newest first.
- One account: `DE89370400440532013000`.
- Columns: `Account Number`, `Booking Date`, `Value Date`, `Transaction Type`, `Payment Reference`, `Beneficiary/Payer`, `Counterparty Account`, `Bank Code`, `Amount`, `Currency`, `Info`.
- **No balance column** and no opening balance. The daily balance has to be calculated ourselves.

## Scenario (from the DOCX)

- Owner: Vincenzo Amalfi. Period: the whole of 2025.
- Net salaries (on the 28th): Vincenzo 3200 (owner's draw), Yusuf Akcit (pizzaiolo) 2350, Rosa Pavese (cook) 2100, Vanessa Paradi (waitress, temporary) 950. Total 8600 EUR/month.
- Taxes and contributions:
  - payroll tax + solidarity surcharge: 880 EUR/month, on the 10th (Frankfurt Tax Office),
  - social insurance: 2650 EUR/month, on the 27th (Techniker Krankenkasse),
  - income tax prepayment (Einkommensteuer): 1150 EUR/quarter (10.03, 10.06, 10.09, 10.12), based on about 30 000 EUR profit from the previous year,
  - trade tax prepayment (Gewerbesteuer): 225 EUR/quarter (15.02, 15.05, 15.08, 15.11).
- Fixed costs: rent 3800 EUR (on the 1st), utilities 650 EUR (on the 10th).
- Capital outflow: silent partnership in the new restaurant of his brother, Luigi, in Zurich. 50 000 CHF in total, i.e. 4166,67 CHF/month on the 15th, which comes to about 4325–4375 EUR.
- Revenue: almost exclusively cards. Debit settled daily, credit paid out 2× a week via Commerzbank Global Pay. Almost no cash.
- June: premises closed for 3 weeks, zero revenue while costs continue. The account goes into overdraft.
- End of year: balance +33 266,35 EUR.

## Chart (PNG)

- Daily balance in 2025. Until May it fluctuates around 0–10k EUR.
- From June (closure) it drops into overdraft. The lowest point is −22 097,60 EUR (03.07).
- It returns to positive in September and rises to about 33.3k EUR at year end.

## Things to watch out for

- The transfers to Switzerland are recorded in the CSV in **CHF** (`Currency = CHF`), not converted to EUR, although the DOCX speaks of a debit in EUR. For the balance they need to be converted at an exchange rate.
- The DOCX and the chart give different overdraft values: −20 098,69 EUR at the end of June according to the DOCX and a minimum of −22 097,60 EUR on 03.07 according to the chart. This is not necessarily a contradiction (different dates), but worth checking when calculating the balance.
- The DOCX gives the file name `pizzeria-amalfi-bank-statement-2025.csv`, but we received the `-v6` version.

## What it is useful for (for the 4 use cases)

- **Liquidity agent**: the June overdraft can be predicted in advance (known closure, fixed costs), and a credit line can be proposed before the holidays.
- **FX hedging agent**: the monthly CHF payments are a currency risk (about 50k CHF a year).
- **Tax agent**: known schedule of quarterly prepayments and monthly taxes. The income tax prepayment is based on about 30k EUR profit, and the year ends with a higher balance, so one can ask about adjusting the prepayments.
- **Investment agent**: a surplus of about 33k EUR at year end that can be invested, while keeping a buffer for the season and June.
